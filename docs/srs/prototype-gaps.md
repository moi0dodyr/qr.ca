# Prototype gaps vs the SRS

These are places where the current prototype (`master` @ `4023e45`) contradicts SRS v1.0 or is missing something. Audited 2026-10-01 against Parts 2a–2c. Each gap is closed by a task brief, or accepted with a decision record. Since 2026-10-06 SRS v2.0 is the source of truth ([0005](../decisions/0005-adopt-srs-v2.md)); the "SRS v1.0 says" column is re-checked against v2 when a gap is next touched.

IDs are `PG-NN` (prototype gap), so they don't clash with upstream's `G-NN` business goals. Differences between Figma and the SRS are tracked separately in [`../design/README.md`](../design/README.md#figma-vs-srs).
