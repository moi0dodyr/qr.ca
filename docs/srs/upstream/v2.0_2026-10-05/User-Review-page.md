## [User] - Review page - rate the business and go to its Google reviews
| User story | As a customer I want to leave a review quickly so that I can share my experience |
| Role | User (not signed in, scanning a code) |
| Platform | Phone browser |
| App map | Not drawn in Figma |
| UI | TBD |
Overview
When the owner chose direct to Google, the scan goes straight to the Google review page. When they chose rating step first, this page opens.
Acceptance criteria
- The page shows a 1–5 rating, then the Leave a Google review button for everyone, whatever the rating.
- Below it, a short private feedback form (Message, optional Email) goes to the owner only.
- The page is phone-first, in English and French, QR.CA-branded like other hosted pages, with no ads.
Google's review policies forbid sending only happy customers to Google, so the Google button is never hidden.
