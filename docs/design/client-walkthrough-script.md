# Client Wireflows Walkthrough Script

A narrative, 15-minute walkthrough script for introducing the client to the complete QR.CA wireflows and features in Figma.

- **Figma File:** [QR.ca – Wireflows](https://www.figma.com/design/heli5YKyvwao7H6XFJQNiY/QR.ca-%25E2%2580%2593-Wireflows?node-id=8193-22054&t=KJlssHOHbF1tChhw-11) (Canvas `8013:3323`, Selection `8193:22054`)
- **Total Call Duration:** ~15 minutes (approx. 7–8 minutes speaking, 7 minutes for discussion and questions)
- **Tone:** Professional, clear, conversational, plain English without heavy jargon

---

## Call Preparation

1. Open the [Figma Wireflows canvas](https://www.figma.com/design/heli5YKyvwao7H6XFJQNiY/QR.ca-%25E2%2580%2593-Wireflows?node-id=8193-22054&t=KJlssHOHbF1tChhw-11).
2. Start zoomed out so the client sees the overall scope and flow connections.
3. Keep cursor movements calm and smooth when panning between sections.

---

## Walkthrough Script

### Introduction (~30 seconds)

*[Start zoomed out slightly so the client sees the whole wireflow canvas]*

> "Hi! Thanks for joining today.
>
> On my screen, you can see the complete user flow map for QR.CA. Today, I want to take you on a quick, high-level tour across the entire journey. We will look at how a user first arrives, how they create and manage their QR codes, how they check their scan data, and how the billing works.
>
> My goal is to keep this under ten minutes so we have plenty of time for your thoughts and questions. Let’s jump right in!"

---

### 1. Landing & Onboarding (~1.5 minutes)

*[Zoom in to the top-left: Landing Page `8045:3711` and Onboarding `8055:3551`]*

> "We start on the public landing page. A new visitor can immediately test the generator and see how easy it is to build a code. When they click to download their code, we invite them to create a free account.
>
> The sign-up is very light—just an email and password, or one click with Google. Right after signing up, a welcome screen greets them and starts their 14-day free trial. We make sure to tell them clearly what is included and how many days they have, without forcing them to enter a credit card upfront. That keeps the first experience completely frictionless."

---

### 2. Main Dashboard & Management (~1.5 minutes)

*[Pan right to the Sidebar `8045:3479` and Dashboard `8045:3708`]*

> "Once they are in, they land on their main dashboard. This is the owner’s command center.
>
> On the left side, the navigation lets them organize codes into custom folders, like marketing campaigns, store locations, or menus. At the top, there is a clear reminder showing their trial status so there are never any surprises.
>
> In the middle is the list of all their QR codes. Each item shows the name, the link, the date, and total scans. From here, owners have quick actions right at hand: they can pause a code, duplicate it, move old codes into an archive folder, or jump straight into editing."

---

### 3. QR Code Creation Wizard (~2 minutes)

*[Pan to the QR Code Creation flow `8045:2816`]*

> "Now let’s look at the core engine of the platform: creating a new QR code. We organized this into a simple step-by-step wizard.
>
> In step one, the user chooses their type. We clearly separate Dynamic codes from Static codes. Dynamic codes are the real power of QR.CA—you can change their destination anytime and track every scan. Static codes are permanent and cannot be changed once printed.
>
> In step two, they add their content. This could be a website, a digital business card, a multi-link page for social media, or a restaurant menu. The form validates the links right away so mistakes are caught before saving.
>
> In step three, they customize the visual design. They can pick shapes, brand colors, and add their logo. We also included a scannability check here, which alerts the user if the contrast is too low before they go to print.
>
> Finally, they download their print-ready file in PNG, SVG, or PDF format.
>
> Right next to this flow, we also have bulk creation for business clients who want to upload a spreadsheet and generate dozens of codes in one shot."

---

### 4. Editing & Updating Printed Codes (~1 minute)

*[Pan briefly down to Edit QR Code `8045:2391`]*

> "One of the biggest selling points for customers is editing. If a restaurant changes its menu or a company changes a web address, they don’t need to reprint their stickers or flyers.
>
> They simply open the edit screen, update the destination or styling, and save. The physical QR code stays exactly the same, and scanners are instantly redirected to the new destination."

---

### 5. Analytics & Performance (~1 minute)

*[Pan to Analytics `8045:3155` and Particular QR Analytics `8045:2575`]*

> "Next is analytics. We provide two levels of insights.
>
> The first is the total account overview. It shows total scans over time, which days performed best, what devices people used, and top cities or provinces across Canada.
>
> The second is a dedicated page for every single QR code. That allows a business to see exactly which physical poster or flyer is driving the most traffic."

---

### 6. Upgrade, Plans & Billing (~1.5 minutes)

*[Pan to Upgrade Pop-up `8053:2117` and Plans & Billing `8045:3051`]*

> "Now, let’s look at how free trial users transition into paying customers.
>
> When an owner wants to upgrade, or when their 14-day trial comes to an end, a simple upgrade pop-up appears. It clearly shows the plan features, transparent pricing in Canadian dollars, and allows quick payment.
>
> For full management, the Plans and Billing page gives owners complete visibility. They can see their active plan, switch between monthly and yearly billing, see when their next charge will happen, and download past receipts as clean PDF invoices with Canadian tax breakdowns. Everything is clear and self-service."

---

### 7. Settings & Integrations (~30 seconds)

*[Pan to Account Settings `8045:3122` and Tracking `8045:3399`]*

> "Lastly, we have the account settings and marketing tools. Along with profile and security settings, we added direct tracking integrations for Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and Meta Pixel. This gives marketing teams the power to retarget customers who scan their codes in the real world."

---

### Conclusion (~30 seconds)

*[Zoom out to show the full canvas again]*

> "And that brings us full circle! Every piece of this flow is connected—from the very first scan on the landing page, to everyday management, all the way to billing and analytics.
>
> I’d love to pause here, hear your thoughts, and answer any questions you have!"

---

## Helpful Phrases for Questions & Interruptions

- **Zooming in closer:**  
  *"Sure, let me zoom in on that specific card for you."*
- **Handling questions on unstyled or missing wireframe details:**  
  *"Good catch. This wireflow focuses on the core structure and layout. In the next visual phase, we will polish all the visual details and edge states."*
- **Steering smoothly back to the flow:**  
  *"Great question, let's keep that in mind as we look at the next screen, because it connects directly to it."*
